Your Gmail accounts

Add a Gmail account

A tiny script runs in that Gmail account, on Google's servers. It checks for codes every minute, even when your phone is off. It only sends out the company name and the code, never your emails. Do this on a computer, with My Codes open there too: it takes about 3 minutes per account.

  1. Give the account a short nickname:
  2. Paste your Telegram bot token (see Settings → Telegram). It's only used to fill in the script, and isn't saved. Copy the script. Your key, nickname and token are already filled in.
  3. Open script.new while signed in to that Gmail account. Tip: on a computer with several Google accounts, use a private window and sign in to just that one.
  4. Select everything in the editor, paste the script over it, and press Save (the disk icon). Name the project My Codes.
  5. In the function menu at the top, pick setup, then press Run.
  6. Press Review permissions and pick the account. Google warns: “Google hasn't verified this app”. That's normal for your own script: press Advanced, then Go to My Codes (unsafe), then Allow.
  7. Wait a few seconds. “My Codes connected” appears in Telegram and in this list. If Run shows an error in red, it tells you exactly what to fix.

Telegram notifications

Push notifications come from your own private Telegram bot. Each one shows the code and the company, with a Copy code button. Code messages delete themselves after 1 hour.

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Name it My Codes. For the username, pick anything ending in bot, e.g. gaber_mycodes_bot.
  3. BotFather replies with a token (looks like 123456789:AAH…). Keep it secret; you paste it when adding a Gmail account.
  4. Tap the link to your new bot and press Start. The scripts need that to find your chat.

Optional: BotFather → /setuserpic to give the bot the My Codes logo.

Use on another device

Scan this with your phone's camera to open My Codes there with your key already set. Then use Firefox's menu → Add app to Home screen (or Install) to get the app icon.
